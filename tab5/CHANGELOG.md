# Tab5 changelog

What the Tab5 port added and fixed, newest first. Test builds are flashing
kits (`tools/make_kit.sh testNN`); each was flashed app-only onto a Tab5 v1.3
and its boot checked. Commit hashes are on the `tab5-port` branch.

## October 2026: Cosmo's pixel UI (test13 to test33)

The Tab5 was redesigned around Cosmo, the Muse's pixel-art avatar, from an
approved mockup: one retro pixel style across the screen, more whimsy, less
"app test GUI".

![The redesigned screen](images/main.png)

### Added

- **Pixel look** (`CONFIG_MUSE_PIXEL_THEME`, on for the Tab5): night-sky
  colours, square borders with notched pixel corners, pixel icons, and pixel
  fonts (Pixelify Sans 19 and 30 for text and titles, Silkscreen 16 for
  labels; both SIL OFL, generated by `esp32/tools/muse/gen_pixel_fonts.sh`).
  Shared by every page through `muse_pixel_style.h`.
- **The dock, rewritten** (`muse_dock.c`): a status strip, Cosmo's name and
  mood line, hold-to-talk that changes with what Muse is doing, a scrolling
  chat with cream and lavender bubbles that wrap to fit, the pocket of quick
  controls, and a full-screen mode that keeps the scenery.
- **Scenery** (`muse_scene.c`): a nebula, stars that twinkle, a ringed
  planet, far-off islands and Cosmo's own floating island, painted in 4 px
  pixels behind him.
- **See-through Cosmo**: the avatar renders with alpha (RGB565A8,
  `muse_pixel_scale_alpha()`), so he stands in the scene instead of a black
  box.
- **Depth and orientation**: the avatar renderer gained `facing` and `walk`
  (`muse_pose_t`). Cosmo turns, his face panel sliding round and hiding when
  he faces away, and his feet step as he walks. Facing front draws exactly
  as before, so all his original animations are kept.
- **Wandering**: when idle Cosmo strolls about his island (smaller further
  back), stargazes, looks around and daydreams in thought bubbles (ramen, a
  star, a note, the moon, a heart). He peeks at the chat when a reply comes,
  and anything else brings him home at once.
- **Work animations** (`muse_props.c`): pixel props drawn the way Cosmo is
  drawn (his outline colour, three-tone shading lit from the top left,
  ordered dithering, a muted palette): a book and magnifier for research, a
  turning globe for a web search, a typewriter with floating notes while he
  writes, an easel with paint for an image, a camera and a soft flash for a
  photo.
- **Muse's activity drives them**: the firmware forwards Muse's
  `agent.status` `activity_text` ("Searching", "Researching", "Generating
  image"...) to the dock, which picks the prop. The codes Muse sends were
  captured on the device and are documented in `NEXT-STEPS.md`.
- **Pictures in a polaroid**: images from Muse (`display.draw_url`) appear
  over the scenery in a pixel polaroid frame.
- **Settings in a pixel window** beside Cosmo, where the pocket opens, with
  every page restyled: pixel icons, notched rows, square switches and
  sliders, a pixel keyboard for passwords.
- **Clock** in the status strip, set by SNTP, in `CONFIG_MUSE_CLOCK_TZ`.
- **Bulletin board** (`main/bulletin.c`, `CONFIG_HOMEHUB_BULLETIN`): notes
  built into the firmware (`tab5/BULLETIN.md`) that Muse reads with
  `bulletin.read`, and `bulletin.post`, with which Muse pins the owner's
  ideas to the board (kept in NVS). Console `>ideas`, `>ideas.clear`.
- **Camera** in the pocket: on/off (remembered), a "snap!" notice while a
  photo is taken, photos the right way up, and the camera powered off at
  boot.
- **Console test commands**: `>dock=...` (pocket, full, demo, osk, walk,
  turn, think, work=KIND, settings[=PAGE]), `>cam`, `>wifi` (the connection
  and its recent drops), `>scan` (passive and active), `>img=URL`, `>chat=`.
- **Screenshots** of the real screen over USB (`esp32/tools/muse/snap.py`,
  `CONFIG_LV_USE_SNAPSHOT`), sized for big screens.

### Fixed

- **Sign-in**: one rejected credential refresh no longer unpairs the Tab5;
  it unpairs only after 4 rejections over at least 30 minutes
  (`main/app.c`, with a host test).
- **Settings crash**: opening the Wi-Fi page overflowed the display task's
  7 KB stack (a deeper widget tree in the window); the Tab5's LVGL task now
  has 16 KB.
- **Text**: chat bubbles and pocket text no longer cut off or bleed; text
  behind the talk button is clear of it (the Muse area is 720×600); the
  keyboard's symbol keys show (the key font falls back to Montserrat).
- **The pixel font's "B"** read as "G" at 19 px; the font script redraws it.
- **The chat scrolls** (its log took no touches).
- **Raw markers** such as `[[..._widget:ID]]` in Muse's replies show as
  "(in the Muse app)".
- **Research vs search**: "Researching" no longer shows the search globe,
  and a new activity text under the same code is no longer dropped.
- **Pictures** were lost in the redesign and are back, framed.
- **Docked overlays**: the face's own captions, reply text and mic meter are
  off beside the dock, whose chat shows them, so Cosmo stays put while he
  answers.
- **Wi-Fi**: passive scans, a drop log, and contract tests updated.
- **Serial tools** no longer reset the Tab5 when they open the port (DTR and
  RTS left off).

### Spoken replies

A free, local voice: Kokoro on the owner's PC with a small filter in front
that raises the pitch for a cartoon voice (`am_liam`), reached over the home
network. See [KOKORO.md](KOKORO.md).
